Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Are NASENI products available for individual retail purchase?

While bulk orders are managed through institutional procurement and authorized distributors, select consumer electronics and home appliances are available via accredited retail partner stores nationwide[cite: 24].

Q2: What maintenance support is provided for solar irrigation pumps and street lights?

All installations include a 3-year maintenance service agreement, with local technician training provided during deployment[cite: 24].

Q3: How can private companies establish a Joint Venture Agreement (JVA) with NASENI?

Entities can submit technical proposals through the NASENI partnership portal. Proposals undergo rigorous technical and financial viability evaluations by our commercial directorate[cite: 24].

Q4: Do NASENI computing devices support international operating systems?

Yes. All NASENI laptops, tablets, and edge servers come pre-validated with Ubuntu Linux, Windows 11 Pro, and custom Yocto builds[cite: 24].

Q5: What certifications do NASENI products hold?

Products carry mandatory compliance certifications from SON, NCC, NAFDAC, NMDPRA, and international standards including ISO 9001 and CE marks[cite: 24].
